## What was achieved in Phase 1

### Website & platform

- **Live website** at **fahadmunafparekh.com** — modern, mobile-friendly design aligned with your brand (navy, gold, professional tutor positioning).
- **Homepage** — hero, credentials, sample lectures, sample notes, registration CTAs, and trust metrics (15+ years, 1,250+ students, 92% A/A*).
- **Course pages** — dedicated pages for:
  - [AS Level Economics](https://fahadmunafparekh.com/courses/as-level) (CAIE 9708)
  - [A2 Level Economics](https://fahadmunafparekh.com/courses/a2-level)
  - [O Level Economics](https://fahadmunafparekh.com/courses/o-level) (2281)
- **Syllabus & sample lectures** — expandable topic lists with **unlocked sample videos** from your recorded lectures; remaining topics shown as locked (full access via Orb-Ed LMS enrolment).
- **Notes library** — study notes from your `public/notes` folders organised by level (AS / A2 / O Level), Micro/Macro, and **Yearly papers** section with tabs.
- **Notes preview** — in-browser PDF preview with **watermark** (no download), zoom in/out for comfortable reading.
- **Quick Learn** — short-form learning clips section.
- **Extra Credit / student life** — student photo gallery auto-built from your image library (class moments, banners, etc.).
- **About & Contact** — bio, location (Alpha College, Karachi), map, WhatsApp, email, and registration links for Oct/Nov 2026 session.
- **Blog** — **12 full articles** published with exam-focused, keyword-rich content (study guides, Paper 4 essays, O Level revision, market failure, elasticity, tuition guide, CAIE vs Edexcel, Oct/Nov 2026 prep, and more).
- **Orb-Ed LMS integration** — direct registration links for O Level, AS, and A2 on course and CTA sections.

### SEO (search engine optimisation) — started

- **On-page SEO** on all main pages — unique titles, meta descriptions, and keywords targeting *“Fahad Munaf Parekh”*, *CAIE 9708*, *Economics tutor Pakistan*, etc.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD)** — Google-readable schema for:
  - Person (Fahad Munaf Parekh)
  - Educational organisation
  - Website
  - Courses (AS / A2 / O Level)
  - Blog articles
- **Sitemap** (`/sitemap.xml`) and **robots.txt** — helps Google discover and index every page and blog post.
- **Canonical URLs & social previews** — correct links when shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram.
- **Blog SEO pipeline** — posts sourced from `blog-posts.md` with target keywords, meta descriptions, and internal linking ready for ongoing content.
- **SEO roadmap documented** — next steps include Google Search Console verification, sitemap submission, and ongoing content (see Phase 2).

### Analytics & marketing foundations

- **Google Analytics 4** installed — measurement ID `G-HE8WG95CGZ` — tracks visitors, pages, and traffic sources once live traffic flows.
- **Google Ads account created** — ready for Phase 2 paid campaigns (not yet spending; account setup complete).
- **Google Business Profile created** — local presence for “Economics tutor Karachi” and name searches; supports maps, reviews, and business posts (ongoing optimisation in Phase 2).

### Technical delivery

- **Static production build** — fast, secure pages suitable for your current hosting (upload `out/` folder after each update).
- **Build workflow** — `npm run build` regenerates blog content and full site for deployment.
- **Brand assets** — logos, banners, watermarked notes, and course imagery integrated across the site.
